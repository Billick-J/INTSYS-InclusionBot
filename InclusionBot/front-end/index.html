    How to use InclusionBot

    • Type your question, or tap one of the suggested questions.
    • Press the microphone to speak your question. Your words appear in the box so you can check them, then press Send.
    • Press Listen under an answer to hear it. Use Pause, Resume and Stop to control it.
    • Turn on Voice (next to the box) to hear every answer automatically.
    • Press Esc to stop the microphone or the reading.
    • Your past chats are listed under Recent chats in the sidebar. Click one to open it again and keep asking, or press the × beside it to delete it.
    • Press Explain simply if the answer is hard to understand.
    • Use Settings to make the text bigger, the colors stronger, or to change the voice, language and reading speed.

    What InclusionBot cannot do

    It gives general information only. It cannot diagnose, give therapy, or help in an emergency. Only the SPED department can confirm admission, eligibility or services.

    Privacy

    Your chats are saved only in this browser on this device (never on the server), so you can open them again later. You can delete them, or turn saving off, in Settings. On a shared computer, turn saving off. Please don't type or say a child's name, assessment reports or other personal records.

    The microphone uses your browser's speech service (in Chrome and Edge your voice is sent to Google or Microsoft to turn it into text), so it needs internet. Reading answers aloud uses the voices on your device.
